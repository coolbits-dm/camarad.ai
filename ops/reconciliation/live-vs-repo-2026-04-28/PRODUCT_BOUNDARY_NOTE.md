# Product Boundary Note

Camarad product direction is the AI workspace for business roles, agents,
provider policy, BYOK, and open-source/self-hosted distribution.

MWR/Vacante Inteligente is not a Camarad product feature.

MWR/Vacante is preserved in this reconciliation only because it is currently
co-hosted in the same live Flask runtime for historical/hosting reasons. The
current task is to preserve existing live behavior, not to migrate or rebrand
MWR/Vacante.

Future task:

- separate MWR/Vacante to cblm-level ownership or a dedicated runtime/module

Current task:

- preserve MWR/Vacante routes, templates, assets, and tests so live parity is
  not broken
- do not remove MWR/Vacante
- do not migrate MWR/Vacante out yet
- do not present MWR/Vacante as part of the Camarad product
